
# Startup Funding Analysis — A Product Case Study

> Where is Indian startup money actually going — and what should investors do about it?

📖 **[Read the full case study on Notion](https://terrific-clownfish-886.notion.site/Startup-Funding-Analysis-A-Product-Case-Study-3636f832ff57808285b8fd4e1487e1fa)**

---

## What This Is

A data-driven product case study analyzing 5,000+ startup funding rounds in India between 2019 and 2025 — across 6 cities, 8 sectors, and 4 funding stages.

The goal: help early-stage investors cut through fragmented newsletters and outdated reports to understand where capital is actually flowing — right now.

---

## The Problem

Early-stage investors in India have no fast, reliable way to track real-time funding trends across cities, sectors, and stages. Most rely on:

- 10+ newsletters weekly
- Manually downloaded Crunchbase CSVs
- WhatsApp angel groups and word of mouth

This project is built around that gap.

---

## Key Findings

- **Bengaluru** captures 52% of all funding — but has the same Seed-to-Series A conversion rate as Mumbai
- **EV and Deeptech** are the only sectors with unbroken growth since 2019
- **The 2022–23 funding winter** was not market-wide — it was a late-stage, high-valuation correction. Seed-stage investors barely felt it
- **Delhi NCR** is underrated for Fintech and D2C; **Hyderabad** is quietly becoming a Deeptech hub
